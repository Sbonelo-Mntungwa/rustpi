# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in rustpi.

This repository doesn't accept pull requests; they are closed automatically. To report a bug, suggest a feature or propose a change, please [open an issue](https://github.com/Sbonelo-Mntungwa/rustpi/issues/new). Include what you expected, what happened, your Raspberry Pi model, and any logs.

You are welcome to fork the project under the terms of the [MIT license](LICENSE).
